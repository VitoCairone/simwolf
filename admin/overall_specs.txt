Required systems

  display
    (1) display terrain and creatures in isometric projection
    (2) render all and only terrain and creatures within a window
    (3) render any motion in the range
    (4) render any pose changes coordinated with motion
    (5) determine sight range and hiding places from every location
    (6) when interface is bound to creature, display fog-of-war where creature cannot see
  movement
    (1) use tile grid to place all creatures
    (2) allow for free movement limited in MVP by simple cooldowns/timers on abilities
    (3) all creatures should be blocking to one another and overlaps forbidden
  species
    (1) MVP has exactly 3 animal species: wolf (predator), deer (prey), rabbit (prey) 
  build
    (1) creature has a width, height, and length.
    (2) creature has a color
    (3) creature has a mass
  chasing
    (1) MVP predator will always chase target or nearest prey, unless unable to eat more
  fleeing
    (1) MVP prey will always flee all predators known to it
  feeding
    (1) MVP consuming food adds the food to own mass as child, otherwise discards it
    (2) Amount of mass which can be consumed at a time is limited
    (3) MVP adults have fixed regular food intake requirement for speed & survival
  combat
    (1) MVP combat resolves immediately for predator on touch
  sensing
    (1) Vision: knowledge of all creatures in vision range
    (2) Hearing: knowledge of all creatures which move within hearing range, MVP no blocking/dampening
    (3) Scent: creatures leave scent gradients on tiles they visit and those nearby
    (4) All senses: MVP creatures in vision or hearing range are identified and located, no uncertainty
  mating
    (1) adult unpaired creatures will pair-bond automatically with opposite sex adult non-relatives
    (2) MVP pairing is random without individual selection
    (3) use simple infant exposure rule to form relative (nonsexual conspecific) lists
  spawning
    (1) mated pairs will result in pregnancy of female
    (2) female gestates, births, and nurses offspring
    (3) male provides food for paired female during gestation, birth, and nursing
    (4) mated pair raises offspring until independance
